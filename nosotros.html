About us · Group 7

Four people,
three objects

Different backgrounds. One feature tree.

We are Group 7 of Conceção e Modelação 3D, a course of the Mestrado em Engenharia e Design de Produto at the Universidade de Aveiro. We come from different backgrounds and different software habits, which is exactly the point of the course.

How we work

Four rules we did not have at the start. Each one cost us a phase to learn.

01

Measure twice

Every hidden rib and screw post goes through the caliper. The details decide if an assembly fits.

Learned in Phase 1
02

Share the numbers

Our lamp fitted the first time because we shared every measurement where one person’s part met another’s.

Learned in Phase 1
03

One master model

Separate parts fail on organic shapes. One master solid, cut into parts, is what saved the iron.

Learned in Phase 2
04

Ask early

Tutoring sessions are not a last resort. Next time we go before we are stuck, not after.

Learned in Phase 2
